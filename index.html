About us

Built around the way cities move.

Who we are

E-Rat is an India-origin mobility initiative founded in 2026, with a vision to build a technology-driven urban mobility ecosystem.

Our mission

Make everyday urban mobility more accessible, reliable and technology-enabled.

Our vision

Build a leading technology-driven mobility ecosystem originating in India.

Our approach

We aim to bring riders, drivers and mobility operations closer through practical technology, thoughtful design and partnerships.

Leadership

Meet the Founder.

FOUNDER & CHIEF EXECUTIVE OFFICER

Jatin Bhatia

Jatin Bhatia founded E-Rat with the ambition to build an India-origin, technology-driven mobility ecosystem. His focus is on bringing riders, drivers and mobility operations closer through accessible and practical technology.

As Founder & CEO, he is guiding the company's early vision, direction and long-term ambition as E-Rat moves through its pre-launch planning stage.
