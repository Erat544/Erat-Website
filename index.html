Built around the way cities actually move.

E-Rat is being developed as a technology-enabled e-rickshaw mobility platform designed to connect riders, drivers and fleet operators.

Our initial focus is Delhi NCR, with a long-term ambition to build a scalable mobility ecosystem originating in India.
