THE OPPORTUNITY

Building for everyday urban journeys.

Short-distance mobility is an important part of daily urban life. E-Rat aims to create a technology-led platform around this segment.

Our Market Focus

Our initial geographic focus is Delhi NCR, with a planned approach to expanding into additional urban markets after validating operations and customer adoption.

Working Market Estimate

Our preliminary working estimate is approximately 4.5–5 lakh e-rickshaws. The geographic scope and underlying data require independent verification before this figure is treated as a confirmed market statistic.

BUSINESS MODEL

Designed around a connected ecosystem.

01

Future advertising opportunities across the mobility ecosystem, subject to execution.
