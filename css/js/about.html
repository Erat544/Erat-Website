OUR STORY

Why E-Rat?

Everyday mobility is made up of thousands of short journeys that connect people to workplaces, schools, markets, public transport and communities.

E-Rat was founded with the ambition to make these journeys easier to access through a connected, technology-enabled e-rickshaw mobility platform.

Starting with a planned focus on Delhi NCR, our long-term ambition is to build an India-origin mobility ecosystem that can grow responsibly across urban markets.

OUR PURPOSE

Mission, vision and values.

Our Vision
